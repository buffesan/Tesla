# GM LS V8 physics-bank proof

This preview is deliberately separate from the Tesla Sound Lab app.

Audio is generated from the open-source Engine Simulator physics model
and converted into a 7 RPM x 3 load seamless-loop bank by
Vangardo's engine-sim-unity-audio generator.

Sources:
- Engine Simulator by AngeTheGreat / Ange Yaghi
  https://github.com/ange-yaghi/engine-sim
- GM LS V8 preset:
  assets/engines/atg-video-2/07_gm_ls.mr
- Offline bank generator by Vangardo:
  https://github.com/Vangardo/engine-sim-unity-audio

Both codebases are MIT licensed.
No paid audio asset is used in this proof.
