# Ferrari California V8 real-recording proof

This is a standalone technical audio proof. It is not yet used by the Tesla Sound Lab app.

Source recording:
- Ferrari California - engine and exhaust sound
- Author: Karla CM
- Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferrari_California_-_engine_and_exhaust_sound_(YouTube_nSDuWqypsNM).opus
- Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (YouTube CC licence at original publication)

This proof is an adaptation: the recording is analyzed for stable engine-speed regions,
cut into whole-cycle loop candidates, circular-crossfaded, pitch-limited and blended
across engine speed. The source remains one and the same vehicle recording throughout.
