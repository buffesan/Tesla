# V5 engine loop source

Source work: **racing car engine sound loops** by domasx2
Original source: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops
License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The six WAV files are mirrored from:
https://github.com/yashimosh/border-run/tree/main/public/sfx/engine

The original OpenGameArt author states the loops were remade from a
public-domain car recording and released as CC0. In this project the
loops are crossfaded and slightly rate-shifted in real time to follow
simulated RPM.
